Search, scoped to who's asking

This is not a login. Pick who is asking, then ask the same question as different people — the point is that the results differ in exactly the way each person's access differs. Two real documents are seeded from a live Drive with their real permissions; two are synthetic, labeled as such, used only to show group- and principal-scoped grants a personal account can't produce on its own.

Permission check runs inside the retrieval query, not after it — ADR-0002.
